# Sample media prompts

Historical record from 23 October 2026. The hero image and panorama were made with an AI image generator; the prompts below are kept word-for-word. The resulting files are in `app/static/images/`. They are invented samples, not photos of the real plots. The sample videos are 8-second animations of still images, not site walkthroughs. Real media from the company will replace all of these.

Land is the focal point, not a road. Calm premium natural photography, realistic detail, no luxury villas or mountains. Wide 2:1 image, no text, no logo, no watermark, no graphics. Invented illustrative sample, not real listing evidence.
